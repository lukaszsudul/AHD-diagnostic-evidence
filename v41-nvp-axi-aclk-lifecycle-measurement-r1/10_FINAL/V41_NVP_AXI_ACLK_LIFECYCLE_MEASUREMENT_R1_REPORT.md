# V41 NVP AXI clock lifecycle measurement R1

## Outcome

The observer-only R1 image built, routed, and programmed successfully. Its free-running counter advanced at a rate consistent with the nominal 62.5 MHz after reboot. The R1 NVP result was a valid functional failure: initialization completed with error, 19 NACKs, zero timeouts, active VCLK, and no SAV or frame progress.

The primary lifecycle cycle-deficit result is **ambiguous**. The programming supervisor correctly captured an elapsed stopwatch tick at the same-process program-return marker, but it terminated after Vivado exited and did not preserve or record the stopwatch epoch needed to relate that elapsed tick to the later absolute T0/T1 stopwatch ticks. No second R1 run was performed. Consequently, no claim that `axi_aclk` paused—or did not pause—is supported by this sample.

The exact formal Phase-2 image was restored once. Its runtime identity, zero/reserved R1 measurement range, PCIe geometry, pinned driver, and final JTAG DONE=1 were all verified. Its contextual NVP baseline again failed (5 NACKs, SAV/frame static).
