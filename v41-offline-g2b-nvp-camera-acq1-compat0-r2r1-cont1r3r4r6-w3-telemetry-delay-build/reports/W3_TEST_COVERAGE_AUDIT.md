No **observed digital assertion failure** appears in the cited final guarded runs. The bounded offline digital checks T02–T31 have direct PASS evidence, including legal terminal overlap while W3 is busy, all 164 entry and 20 group slots, a real-master compound NACK/timeout record, and same-kind validity/context. T01 is PARTIAL until the final source commit/tree and guarded build inputs are fixed. These two are release-stage identity gates, not an observed RTL/test failure. Physical-master cadence, natural 2^32 traffic, analog behavior and DUT capture are outside the bounded offline evidence. Full W3 qualification must not be claimed from this report alone.
