The completed-command model returns each command after 80 scanner clock cycles, much faster than a physical I2C transaction. The 82-error scan stresses the bounded writer and record freeze at that deterministic cadence, but cannot by itself prove the physical master's worst-case command-rate envelope, pin-level ACK/NACK, SCL waiting, waveform equivalence or an NVP physical cause. The ON-pause abort and preemption cases check digital ownership and cleanup, not pin-level ACQ contention. Separate real-master benches and rate/inference evidence cover those gates.

The armed partial scan completed 37 entries and accepted 54 master attempts: the historical 51-operation base through failed Bank5 select plus three recovered entry retries. The next clean scan added exactly 105 accepted attempts. After reset, the unarmed rapid-restart case repeated a partial modeled scan and completed a fresh 105-operation scan. This bench does not reproduce the historical 51-attempt trace itself; the separate Bank5 pin-level replay covers that case.

T24 remains **partial** for previous-group identity across a group boundary; the exact entry0 current-group verify delta is checked against independent model timestamps. T25 remains **partial**: this bench checks incomplete-attempt identity, generation independence, retention, visible unarmed writer overlap and an accelerated scan-attempt/payload-revision wrap through a genuine scan. The near-wrap values were injected into private scalar registers at safe idle; this is a synthetic boundary setup, not evidence from 2^32 natural admissions. Transaction-sequence wrap and legacy generation wrap are not exercised. T27 does not exercise same-cycle finalization/capture at the theoretical maximum burst. T22 wait/timeout last-cycle and 11-bit saturation belong to the separate real-master bench, not this completed-command model.
