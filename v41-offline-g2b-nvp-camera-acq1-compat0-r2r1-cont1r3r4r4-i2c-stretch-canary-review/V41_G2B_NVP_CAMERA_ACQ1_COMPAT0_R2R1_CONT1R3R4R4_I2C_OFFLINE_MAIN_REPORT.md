# AHD v41 — CONT1R3R4R4-I2C-OFFLINE

## Decision

The offline receiver experiment found **no false ACK-to-NACK decision in the 214 executed cases**. All 214 cases reached completion markers and passed independent expected-outcome/pin checks; 20/20 named coverage checks were executed. The observed 72 raw5 SCL timeouts are the unchanged master's configured finite-low timeout, not raw DATA_NACK. Four raw2/raw4 NACKs occurred only after intentionally illegal early ACK release; four absent-ACK controls returned the matching raw causes. No RTL ACK defect was reproduced. The scientific classification is `PASS_NO_FALSE_NACK_IN_EXECUTED_GRID`, **not** a proof for every I²C timing, this specific chip's undocumented stretch behavior, or the historical physical event.

The experiment also exposes an important policy limit: 52 of the 72 local timeouts had otherwise digitally generic-valid ACK/stretched-clock timing, and 20 had additional analog-input-delay uncertainty. The local 1250-cycle/nominal20 µs wait budget is not a vendor-certified maximum. The NVP6134C Rev1.0 data sheet does not state a maximum stretch in the reviewed sections and labels SCL as an input, so the model does not show that this device actually stretches the physical bus. The prior 25-scan/11-event hardware evidence remains inherited and `NOT_ASSESSED_CAMPAIGN_INCOMPLETE`; it contains neither the failed command's sticky raw cause nor pin timing. No offline run can assign the assembled-board root cause.

## Exact experiment

Frozen design source: `lukaszsudul/FPGA_AHD` commit `09cd7cbb426027acaefd0cf3989579b80a451f3a`, tree `c6be008ddc387c1f43eefa35d4fed0e2ccb968db`. XSim 2025.2 SW6299465 compiled only the byte-exact frozen master, scanner and manifest package as design files, with task-owned testbenches. The actual profile is 62.5 MHz FPGA, nominal 25 kHz I²C, `DIVIDER=1250`, SCL wait threshold1250 cycles and bus-idle threshold62500 cycles. The 214-case matrix and source/timing rules were preregistered and hashed before execution. No refinement cases were run. Results split into 114 digitally generic-valid, 50 digital sensitivity/analog-unverified, and 50 deliberately invalid/stress cases. Correctly timed ACK plus 256-cycle extra low passed a full simulated 82-entry/105-transaction scan. A 2500-cycle hold at the same Bank5 select became raw5/timeout1, scanner detail `0x0005FF04`, 37 valid entries and verified restore; no historical generation was simulated.

The real master has structurally symmetric SCL/SDA two-flop and candidate/count filters. The SCL timeout counter runs only when SCL has been released but is not yet filtered high; the ACK is sampled on a later high-state divider tick, not at the raw pin rising edge. At one nominal phase, 1240 extra cycles completed and 1248 timed out. Measured raw-to-filtered latency in the bounded digital grid was 64–80 ns SCL and 72–80 ns SDA low. These are simulation timestamps, not PCB analog rise or chip threshold measurements. The model's cancelled-rise negative test and six independent checker-corruption tests passed. Three disclosed checker amendments consumed the allowed harness-revision budget. A separate post-run **CSV export-only** repair rejoined the immutable case matrix with 214 hashed logs and traces; it did not alter stimuli or the checker verdict. The original exported table is retained privately.

## Raw status, canary and documents

The active master's 4-bit raw cause is cleared on acceptance of a new command. SCAN1 restore/verify can therefore overwrite the live cause before the host sees a hard-stop result. Active MMIO `0x1202C` exposes a mapped first-error detail, not a sticky failed-command raw cause; the incomplete scan does not publish a new complete snapshot. Bank0/0x80 has a documentary default `0x00` and configured `0x0F` and appears in SCAN1, but the stored snapshot after a failed scan is stale and the data sheet does not identify power-on versus RESETB versus software-reset defaults. A fresh fixed one-register post-error read does not exist in the current image. Its disposition is `READABLE_BUT_NOT_RESET_DISCRIMINATING`; Bank1/0xCA and 0xCD have no active read path.

Nextchip NVP6134C Rev1.0 (93-page local PDF SHA-256 `301FF799A101B0DBDF6CD946EEAD0C1EDC67D07FFEAC7E65FA8C6AE82C316E46`) §8.4 p90 gives 100 kHz typical/400 kHz max SCL and relevant low/high/setup/rise/fall figures, not a maximum clock-stretch or Bank0xFF settle rule. Pin Table1.1 p10 marks SCL input and SDA bidirectional. [NXP UM10204 Rev7.0](https://www.nxp.com/docs/en/user-guide/UM10204.pdf) §3.1.9, §6.1 Table11/footnotes supply the *generic Standard-mode* timing rules; the official web rendering was reviewed but a local NXP PDF hash is not claimed. The pinned sample transport adds 200 or 300 µs post-write delay in two available branches while ignoring the helper return; its external/platform implementation and any adapter-level retries are absent. Repeated bank-write code is not a vendor idempotence guarantee.

The reviewed KiCad files identify a **Rev0.1 / 2020** design snapshot, not the serial-linked assembled DUT. That netlist connects NVP RESETB to FPGA R17 with a schematic 100 kΩ pull-up to VDD3D and capacitor to ground. SCL/SDA map FPGA T17/T18 to NVP pins64/63 with schematic 4.7 kΩ pull-ups to Vcco. No translator/series component or dedicated probe point appears on those net entries. Actual fitted parts, Vcco/VDD3D voltages, RESETB waveform, ground/probe access and PCB revision remain unverified. Neither the schematic nor the NVP document supports assuming a safe physical observation point or explaining a sporadic ACK.
