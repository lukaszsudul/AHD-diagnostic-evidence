The reference source's `docs/nvp6134_datasheet_release_v03_161025.pdf` is for a differently suffixed part and is not substituted for the NVP6134C Rev1.0 source. Absence of a `sleep` at a `video.c` call site alone would be misleading: the available `HI_I2C`/`I2C_INTERNAL` helper implementations have 200/300 µs post-write delays. Their reason is not documented in these sources. No source proves message retry at the absent external transport layer; no automatic re-execution of an entire initialization block was identified in this bounded call path. This review does not authorize changing FPGA timing or retry policy.
