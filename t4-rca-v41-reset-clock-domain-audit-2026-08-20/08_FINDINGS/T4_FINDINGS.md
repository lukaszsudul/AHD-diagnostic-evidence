# Findings

Primary: `T4_INCONCLUSIVE_MISSING_EXACT_ARTIFACTS` (T4-F).

The protected NVP RTL/table/XDC are byte-identical. Both logical and compared implemented clocks are nominally 62.5 MHz and derive from the PCIe GT/pipe clock family. No v41-specific reset fan-in reaches the NVP functional state. A timing-only explanation cannot be tested from preserved timestamps, and the exact v41 Phase-2 routed DCP/generated-IP lifecycle is missing. Therefore T4-A/B/C/E are not defensible. A v41 implementation/I/O margin difference remains plausible, but unproven.
