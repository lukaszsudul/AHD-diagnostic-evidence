I2C engine. The 25-kHz bench changes only the testbench generic and its bounded
test watchdog. Its exact SETUP-to-done span is 651,882 base-clock cycles at the
1-MHz simulation clock: 31,042 inclusive-divider tick intervals times 21
cycles. The 50-kHz control span is 341,462 cycles: 31,042 times 11. These
measurements independently confirm the source calculator's 31,043-action model
and production lifecycle-counter reconstruction.

bytes, and post-table readback/restore order. Only the tick spacing differs.
