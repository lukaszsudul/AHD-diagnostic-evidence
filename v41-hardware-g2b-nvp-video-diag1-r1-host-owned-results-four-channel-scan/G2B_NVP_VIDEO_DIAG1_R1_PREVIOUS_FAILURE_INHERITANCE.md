# Previous DIAG1 failure inheritance

Inherited DIAG1 post-opt utilization was 22,306/20,800 LUT (107.240%),
23,674/41,600 FF (56.909%), and 26.5/50 BRAM (53.000%). The accepted defect was
the 4,096-bit, 128x32 asynchronous, resettable on-chip result history. The NVP
register contract, I2C ownership, whitelist, scan protocol, and 16/16 prior
simulation gate were carried forward without re-audit.
