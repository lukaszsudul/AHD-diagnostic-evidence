# Candidate 1: telemetry writer service envelope

Source: `09cd7cbb426027acaefd0cf3989579b80a451f3a`, tree `c6be008ddc387c1f43eefa35d4fed0e2ccb968db`. This is an offline design proof, not hardware sign-off.

The fixed master is unchanged (`rtl/v41/nvp_i2c_fixed_master.sv`, SHA-256 `8C916DD7E3967AB22FF0ED90D9BC4533FA50ADE3FB0F4EB620D26B35E93363BF`). Effective profile parameters are `CLK_HZ=62,500,000`, `I2C_HZ=25,000`, `DIVIDER=1,250`, `TICK_CYCLES=1,251`, `SCL_TIMEOUT_CYCLES=1,250`, and `BUS_IDLE_TIMEOUT_CYCLES=62,500`. The complete profile build must confirm these effective parameters and the 62.5 MHz clock constraint.

The master first enters `LL_WAIT_IDLE` after command acceptance. Even with an already idle bus it requires 1,251 stable clock cycles before `LL_START_A`; with a non-idle bus the timeout is longer. Thus no accepted command can complete inside the telemetry writer's 12-clock upper bound. Later legal states and clock-stretch/abort paths add time, not shorten this bound.

An entry completion captures the already-timestamped ten-word record into a 10-word buffer and raises one flush pulse on that edge. The writer accepts the pulse on the next edge and writes one 32-bit word per edge for ten edges. A group verification similarly stages five words and drains in seven edges. The next entry may start while a prior write is draining, but its record buffer is not replaced until its completion. A group change requires two further whole I2C commands before the next entry completion. Consequently one buffer and one writer are lossless for the verified real-master envelope, including 82 recovered first-attempt failures: retries lengthen, not shorten, the interval between terminal entry completions.

The accelerated directed fixture uses a 20-clock completion latency, above the 12-clock writer bound and far below the hardware minimum. Its clean, multi-event and 82-event scans compared all 870 words with the exact failed-parent RTL and preserved command-acceptance cycle equivalence. A faster synthetic fixture is **outside** the fixed-master contract; overlapping flushes assert `telemetry_write_service_fault`, then `telemetry_overflow`, and prevent a complete/frozen PASS. They do not backpressure the I2C command path. Further directed overflow and interface tests remain required before sign-off.

The RAM is 1,024×32, with one synchronous read and one synchronous write port. Group words occupy indices 32–81, entry words indices 128–947. Header, gaps, unaligned addresses and the two implementation-identity words never access RAM. A complete/frozen generation requires 82 committed entries, 10 committed groups, no pending writer work, 82 recorded exposures and no fault/overflow. RAM bits are not reset; earlier-generation bytes cannot pass the host's complete/generation gate.
