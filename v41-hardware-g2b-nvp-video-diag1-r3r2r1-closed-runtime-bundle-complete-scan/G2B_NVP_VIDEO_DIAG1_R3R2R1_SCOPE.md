
# Scope

the previously unstarted 4x4 diagnostic matrix. It does not change FPGA RTL, XDC,
IP, bitstream, transport ABI, PRODUCT MMIO, SSOT, or persistent NVP state.

Camera pixels, primary captures, UYVY frames, PNGs, thumbnails, native binaries,
driver binaries, FPGA bitstreams, DCPs, and credentials are excluded from public
evidence.
