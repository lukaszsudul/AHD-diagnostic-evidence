
# BT.656 availability decision

CH1 and CH3 were `BT656_READY_PRE_RESET_AND_POST_RESET` in 4/4 rounds. CH2 and
CH4 had active VCLK but zero SAV before and after reset in 4/4 rounds. Reset
recovered zero routes and regressed zero routes. The complete matrix therefore
proves two consistently available and two consistently unavailable BT.656 routes.
