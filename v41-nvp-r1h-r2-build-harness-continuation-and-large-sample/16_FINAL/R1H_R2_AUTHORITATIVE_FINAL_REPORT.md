## Evidence vocabulary

- **FACT** is an exact file, hash, Git, process-accounting or receipt value.
- **SOURCE-DERIVED FACT** follows directly from exact source identity.
- **TOOL-DERIVED FACT** is emitted by Vivado or its simulation frontends.
- **INFERENCE** states a bounded interpretation and its evidence limit.
- **NOT RUN** means the stage was not invoked.
- **NOT AVAILABLE** means the requested result cannot exist because its stage
  did not run. It is never a numeric zero or a PASS.
- **HISTORICAL ONLY** means the value comes from R7/R1h history and was not
  freshly observed during R1h-R2.

raw LUTs (7.43%), so it does not meet the owner-mandated 10% headroom gate.

24,576 index bits are no longer a large FDRE bank, and the failed-record
payload is no longer RAM64M/RAMD64E—but the resulting whole design still lacks
the required LUT safety margin by 535 Slice LUTs. This is a resource-gate
finding, not a placement or timing result because implementation did not run.
