# Reachability and ambiguity decision

Three claims must remain separate:

1. `DATA_NACK_MAPPING_TO_0xA_CONFIRMED`: frozen source mapping and T02/T15 establish that a failed Bank5-select write with raw master `CAUSE_DATA_NACK=0x4`, timeout=0, is published by SCAN1 as `0x0005FF0A`.
2. `DATA_NACK_SELECT_PATH_REPRODUCES_SIGNATURE`: T02's 26 complete and acknowledged generations followed by that injected failure reproduced every retained SCAN1 field of the historical partial receipt, including the unchanged generation, 37 valid/zero failed/zero retried, index 36, 51 commands, flags, entry/exit Bank0 and post-ACK idle. T15 independently obtained raw 4 from the unmodified master by withholding the data-byte ACK on resolved open-drain pins at `0xFF=0x05`; the scanner then produced the same relative signature with prior generation 0. It did not pretend to recreate the historical absolute generation.
3. `HARDWARE_DATA_NACK_DIRECTLY_OBSERVED=NO`: the historical failed-command raw master cause and pin sample were not retained. SCAN1 code `0xA` is a many-to-one mapping, not a raw I2C trace. No simulation establishes which electrical condition caused the real failure.

Within the inspected **ordinary legal master paths**, raw DATA_NACK on the select data byte is the only path that gives both error nibble `0xA` and the observed 51-command select-stage accounting with successful restore. Select WADDR/REGADDR NACK and SCL/bus-idle timeout share 51 commands but publish distinct nibbles. Verify-read RADDR NACK or bank mismatch would need 52 commands and publish other nibbles. An entry-read failure affects failed/retried counters and its target register. A restore failure retains the earlier first error but changes count/flags/status and locks out. Scanner's internal default state emits `0xA` at bank/register zero, not `0x05/0xFF`.

This bounded uniqueness does **not** imply physical uniqueness. T11 demonstrates that a synthetic `success=0,timeout=0,raw=0` completion supplied to the scanner at the select point also publishes nibble `0xA` with the same relative signature. The master's illegal-state default/abort path can theoretically produce this interface combination because command acceptance first clears the cause, but no normal state sequence to it was shown. Raw 5 or 6 with timeout 0 and reserved causes 7–15 also map to `0xA` in the scanner but were reachable only by deliberately inconsistent mock outputs, not a documented ordinary master exit. Interface corruption or reset/timing faults were not formally excluded. These are assumptions/alternatives, not competing evidence of a real fault.

T03 supplied a further ambiguity within raw DATA_NACK itself: either the modeled bank remained at Bank1 or it accepted Bank5 despite missing ACK. Both were restored to entry Bank0 and yielded indistinguishable final SCAN1 fields. Thus the successful final restore proves final bank equality, not the intermediate bank state at the missing ACK. The Level B slave intentionally left the bank unchanged on NACK; it is a digital stimulus model, not an NVP vendor model.

An actual high SDA sample in the master's data ACK slot could arise from device non-acknowledgement or from timing, stretching, electrical noise, voltage threshold, sample alignment, line contention or another physical condition. None is proven by the retained receipt. No bank-select retry, timing workaround, firmware change, reliability qualification, camera-format claim or real-frame claim follows from this offline task.
