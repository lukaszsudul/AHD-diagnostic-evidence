# Recovered REGADDR_NACK records — existing evidence only

The sealed archive was verified before extraction; each of its 26 completed raw records was re-decoded with unchanged `host/cont1r3/telemetry.py` and `host/scan1/decoder.py` from source commit `09cd7cbb426027acaefd0cf3989579b80a451f3a`. Every raw record matched its private manifest size/SHA-256, legacy/telemetry hashes, exact scanner manifest, frozen schema identity, group/entry counts, generation, command accounting, coherent publication and public progress row. The failed next attempt is **not** in the archive and was not fabricated from the previous generation's payload.

The single control scan is generation 1, 82 entries, 10 groups, 105 commands, zero recovered events. The 25 complete campaign scans are generations 2–26 and have 2,050 first-attempt entry exposures and 250 group records. Fifteen of those campaign scans were clean; ten contained 11 recovered events. With control included the denominators are 2,132 exposures and 260 group records. The scanner hard-stop receipt is separate, incomplete and excluded from those counts. These are censored descriptive counts, not a 1,000-scan rate.

All 11 re-decoded events are first-attempt read `REGADDR_NACK` (`raw=2`, `decoded=2`), followed by successful single retries and complete scan publications. The public summary and private JSONL rows match the raw-derived values. The event table `REGADDR_EXISTING_EVENTS.csv` retains scan label, generation, bank/register, group/position, raw and decoded first cause, retry result, transactions since group verification, FPGA tick delta and its nanosecond conversion. Conversion authority is the frozen 62,500,000 Hz, 64-bit counter; the floor conversion is `ticks × 1,000,000,000 / 62,500,000`, exactly 16 ns per tick in this profile. No missing tick was invented from a public CSV.

Importantly, `campaign-0022`, entry 19, group 3, position 8, is retained. The 11 events span groups 3–8: group event counts are 1, 2, 3, 2, 1, 2 respectively. This rules out describing the **observed recovered events** as confined to the first few positions or to Bank5 alone. The events show intermittent recoverable register-address ACK loss during completed reads; the stopped transaction was a write-data/select-stage failure inferred only through SCAN1's mapped error code. These classes need not have one electrical cause. The preregistration SHA-256 remains `18CDD7FBC67ABDB799475CE509BC686C3A2CD0E7153BC4E44910ABC809B5DF8C` and its interpretation is unchanged: `NOT_ASSESSED_CAMPAIGN_INCOMPLETE`.

Offline test T17 verified all 26 raw records and reconciled all 11 events and 2,050 campaign exposures; T18 exercised independent signature and raw-hash rejection. The private archive and full raw payloads were not published.
