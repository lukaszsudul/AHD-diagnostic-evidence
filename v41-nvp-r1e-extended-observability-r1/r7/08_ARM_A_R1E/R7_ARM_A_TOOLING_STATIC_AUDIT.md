The terminal-safe receipt is generated immediately after the accepted Arm-A
program and independent immediate DONE=1. It intentionally precedes the wait,
reboot, driver, and telemetry gates so a later infrastructure-invalid Arm A
does not prevent an otherwise safe Arm-B restoration. A valid Arm-A receipt is
created only after the complete telemetry and final-DONE gates pass.
