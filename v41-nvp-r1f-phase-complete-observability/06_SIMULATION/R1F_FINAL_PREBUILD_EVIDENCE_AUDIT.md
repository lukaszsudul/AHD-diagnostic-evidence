## Scope distinctions

The exact production pre-init equivalence and physical open-drain arbitration
are current RTL simulations. The integrated producer matrix, logger, serial,
map and focused tri-phase engine are also current RTL simulations. Exact
29/10000, Bernoulli and clustered large probe sequences are frozen executable
reference-model tests paired with current focused RTL mechanics. The combined
classification is:

~~~text

    PASS_COMBINED_CURRENT_RTL_AND_FROZEN_REFERENCE_MODEL
P8_UNCONDITIONAL_MISSING_CASES=NONE
~~~

Earlier failed or superseded logs remain preserved but are not accepted-log
candidates in this table.
