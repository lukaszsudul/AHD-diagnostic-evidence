# PCB-PIN-0 SSOT Impact

No SSOT change is required by this feasibility audit. The proposed pins are not promoted, accepted, or substituted into `project-current-state`, G-track, R-track, RTL, XDC, or the Vivado product project.

If the Owner accepts the new PCB pinout, a later governed META update will be required together with the corresponding schematic, RTL top-level, XDC, timing constraints, and implementation evidence. That later update should explicitly record the mandatory 27 MHz C13-to-D13 correction, the CH2 T15/U15 decision, Bank 14/15 VCCO, MPP retirement, and J18 schematic disposition.
