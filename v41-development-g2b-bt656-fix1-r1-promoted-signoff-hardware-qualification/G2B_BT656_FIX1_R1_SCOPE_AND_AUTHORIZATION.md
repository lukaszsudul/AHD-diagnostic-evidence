# Scope and authorization

The run changed only a fresh task-local external sign-off/recovery harness. It was authorized for exact routed-DCP recovery, complete promoted sign-off, one PRODUCT bitstream, one SRAM programming, one warm reboot, one finite primary-only rolling capture, one conditional post-target transport reset, normal unload and evidence publication. Flash, power-cycle, retries, 60-second capture, two-channel capture, NVP/VDO changes, RTL/XDC/IP edits, SSOT edits and release creation were not performed.
