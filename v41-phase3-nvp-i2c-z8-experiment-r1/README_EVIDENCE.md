At the first reproduced write-address NACK, SCL reached qualified HIGH and the
master had released SDA and SCL. SDA stage 1, stage 2, and the filtered value
were HIGH at early, midpoint, and late correct-phase observations. The Z8
midpoint sample was valid and consumed NACK. Therefore ACK-phase relocation was
not sufficient to recover this reproduced transaction.

This result does not prove a sole device, electrical, rail, reset, mounting, or
signal-integrity cause. Raw analog measurement remains unavailable.
