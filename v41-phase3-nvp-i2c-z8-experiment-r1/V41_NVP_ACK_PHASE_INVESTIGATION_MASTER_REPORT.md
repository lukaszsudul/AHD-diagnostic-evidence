# v41 NVP ACK-phase investigation master report

## Final outcome

Stage A published the independent-review erratum. Stage B's improved R2
observer proved a real digital NACK during a valid SCL-HIGH ACK opportunity.
Stage C's single Z8 midpoint experiment reproduced the same real digital NACK;
moving the ACK sample was therefore insufficient for the observed event.

The exact formal Phase-2 image was restored after both Stage B and Stage C.
The new read-only closure audit independently verified that the formal image is
still active. Phase 3 remains paused and no production correction was
integrated.
